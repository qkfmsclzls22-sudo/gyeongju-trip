## Platform
web

## Purpose and audience
Gyeongju Trip is a local cultural tourism company. This website helps visitors browse docent-led Gyeongju tours and continue to the existing tour details and booking flows. School, company, MICE and private group visitors can request custom planning. These facts come from the existing site.

## Product truth
- Three existing tours: National Gyeongju Museum, Bulguksa and Silla Starlight Night Walk. Prices and operation facts remain authoritative in lib/tours.ts and existing tour pages.
- Homepage tools include tour comparison, companion/interest suggestions, an optional date note and a browser-local saved-tour notebook. A notebook is not a reservation or confirmed itinerary.
- Preserve account, payments, existing tour links, group enquiry, galleries and mobile navigation.

## Owner's design commitments
The owner selected STAYFOLIO's campaign/editorial reference, asked for motion typography, and prefers Gyeongju imagery and brand atmosphere. The owner asked to use their watercolor artwork and delegated composition decisions. The latest request explicitly authorizes a bold transformation of the UI, image color and typography, prioritizing spaciousness over a cramped feeling. Existing original artwork remains available; derivatives must be traceable.

## Release and accessibility
Preview deployment for owner review; do not merge or promote production without authorization. Maintain mobile layouts, semantic controls, keyboard focus and reduced-motion support. No invented reviews, ratings or business claims.
